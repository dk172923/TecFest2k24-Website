Connection Club

The Connection Game is a thought-provoking challenge where players link diverse elements to unveil hidden connections. Sharpen your wit and strategic thinking in this engaging pursuit of unraveling mysteries through clever associations.

Rules:

  • Approximately 2 to 4 members per team is the limit.
  • Participants will have 30 seconds to answer.
  • No discussion with other teams while playing the game.
  • Team will be disqualified if bullying and distractions occur.
  • The participants who score the highest points in Round 1 will advance to Round 2
