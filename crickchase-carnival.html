CrickChase Carnival

Welcome to CrickChase Carnival, our Box Cricket Extravaganza! Gather your team of four for a thrilling cricket experience. With a limited playing area and a defined set of rules, this fast-paced event promises non-stop excitement. Get ready to showcase your cricket skills and compete against other teams in the ultimate battle for victory!

Rules:

  • Each team must consist of 4 members.
  • Every match is a knockout stage.
  • Hitting a six is considered a wicket.
  • Per match consists of 5 overs.
  • Leg side and off side are considered as 2 runs.
  • Straight is considered as 4 runs.
